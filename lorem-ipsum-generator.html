What is Lorem Ipsum?


Lorem Ipsum is scrambled Latin placeholder text that designers and developers have been using since the 1500s. An unknown printer took a passage from Cicero's De Finibus Bonorum et Malorum and rearranged it to create a type specimen book. Five centuries later, it is still the default way to fill a layout before the real copy exists.


The reason it survived is simple: it looks like real text. A page full of "text text text text" looks wrong at a glance, but Lorem Ipsum has natural word lengths and sentence rhythm. That makes it ideal for mockups, wireframes, and design reviews where the eye should be on layout rather than meaning.


This generator gives you five distinct styles — Classic Latin, Tech/Developer, Business/Corporate, Hipster/Artisan, and Nature/Science — so the placeholder text can match the tone of the project. It also outputs plain text, HTML, Markdown, or JSON, depending on where the text is going.

SEO note: never publish Lorem Ipsum to a live page





Lorem Ipsum is a design tool, not content. If you leave placeholder text on a production page, search engines will index it. Your page can rank for gibberish instead of the keywords you are targeting, and a page full of placeholder text may be treated as unfinished or low-quality.




Staging environments are not safe either. Crawlers do not know the difference between staging and production, and lorem-filled staging pages have ended up indexed more than once. If you use Lorem Ipsum for mockups, password-protect the staging environment or add a noindex meta tag. The rule is simple: Lorem Ipsum is for mockups, wireframes, and design reviews. Real content goes live.
