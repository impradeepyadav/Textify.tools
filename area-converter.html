Why use this area converter?

Area measurements are used in countless fields: square meters for floor plans, acres for land, square feet for real estate in the US, hectares for agriculture, and square miles for geography. This tool makes it simple to convert between all of them instantly.

  • Instant conversion – results update as you type or change units.
  • 10 different units – covers metric, imperial, and land measurement units.
  • Copy result – one click copies the converted value.
  • Swap units – quickly reverse the conversion direction.
  • 100% private – calculations happen locally in your browser.

How to convert area units

1. Enter a value

Type any number into the value box.

2. Choose units

Select the unit you're converting from and the unit you want to convert to.

3. Get result

The converted value appears instantly. Use the copy button or swap direction.
