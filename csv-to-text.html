What does this CSV to Text converter do?

This free online CSV to text converter takes comma-separated values (CSV) data and turns it into clean, readable plain text. Instead of raw rows with commas and quotes, you get text formatted with your chosen delimiter — tab, space, pipe, or anything else. It's the fastest way to turn spreadsheet exports, database dumps, or API responses into human-readable text.

Unlike basic converters that just replace commas with tabs, this tool gives you full control over the conversion. You can choose the input delimiter (or let it auto-detect), pick any output delimiter, strip surrounding quotes, align columns for readability, trim whitespace, and skip empty lines. It handles quoted fields correctly — including fields that contain commas, newlines, or escaped double quotes inside them.

Everything runs locally in your browser. Your CSV data never leaves your device, there's no signup, and there's no built-in limit beyond a browser safety guard that prevents runaway output from freezing your tab.

How to convert CSV to text (3 simple steps)

  1. Paste your CSV: Drop your CSV data into the input box above. It can be comma-separated, tab-separated, semicolon-separated, or anything else. The tool auto-detects the delimiter by default.
  2. Choose your settings: Pick an output delimiter (tab is the default, but you can use space, comma, pipe, or custom). Enable "Align columns" if you want a neat text table, or "Strip quotes" if you want clean values without surrounding quotes.
  3. Click Convert to Text: The output appears instantly. Copy it to your clipboard or download it as a .txt file.

That's it. No uploads, no waiting, no server round-trips.

Delimiter guide: choosing the right separator

The delimiter determines how fields are separated in the output. The right choice depends on what you're going to do with the text.

Delimiter Best for Example output
Tab Readable columns, spreadsheets, TSV files Alice   30   New York
Space Simple readable text, logs, reports Alice 30 New York
Comma Preserving CSV structure as plain text Alice, 30, New York
Pipe Delimited text files, data exports Alice | 30 | New York
Semicolon European-style CSV, readable lists Alice; 30; New York
Custom Any string, like →, ###, or :: Alice → 30 → New York

If you're building a readable text table, use Tab and enable Align columns. If you're preparing data for a script or log file, Pipe or Comma works well. For maximum readability in plain text, Space is the simplest choice.

CSV vs TSV vs plain text: what's the difference?

CSV (Comma-Separated Values) uses commas to separate fields. It's the most common format for spreadsheet exports and data interchange. However, commas inside data values need to be wrapped in quotes, which can make the raw data hard to read.

TSV (Tab-Separated Values) uses tabs instead of commas. It's cleaner to read and parse because tabs rarely appear inside data values. This is often the best choice when converting to plain text.

Plain text has no structured delimiter — it's just human-readable text. When you convert CSV to text, you're essentially choosing a delimiter that makes the data readable and useful for your specific purpose.
