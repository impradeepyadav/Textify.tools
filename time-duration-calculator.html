What does this Time Duration Calculator do?

This free online time duration calculator handles three of the most common time-math problems in one place. It finds the exact duration between two clock times, it adds or subtracts a duration from a starting time, and it calculates the total span between two full dates and times.

The Between Two Times tab is the one most people need. Enter a start time and an end time, optionally deduct a break in minutes, and you get the net duration in hours and minutes, plus decimal hours and total minutes — the exact formats payroll and invoicing systems expect. Overnight shifts (where the end time is earlier than the start time) are detected and handled automatically.

Everything runs locally in your browser. No server, no signup, no tracking. Your times never leave your device.

How to use the Time Duration Calculator

Between Two Times

  1. Enter a start time and an end time. Both use the 24-hour format for precision.
  2. If you took a break during the period, enter the break length in minutes. Leave it at 0 for no deduction.
  3. Leave Overnight set to "Auto-detect" and the calculator will handle it if the end time is earlier than the start time. Force it on or off if you want to override the detection.
  4. Click Calculate Duration. The net duration appears as hh:mm:ss, with decimal hours and total minutes shown separately.

Add / Subtract Time

  1. Enter a starting time.
  2. Choose Add or Subtract.
  3. Enter the duration in hours, minutes and seconds.
  4. Click Calculate Result. Midnight crossover is handled automatically — for example, 23:00 + 2 hours = 01:00 the next day.

Date & Time Range

  1. Enter a full start date and time.
  2. Enter a full end date and time.
  3. Click Calculate Span to get the total gap in days, hours, minutes and seconds, plus total hours, minutes and seconds.

How time duration is calculated

All three modes reduce to the same arithmetic: convert everything to seconds, do the math, then convert back. Here's how each mode works under the hood.

Mode Formula Handles
Between two times end − start (− break) If negative, add 24 hours (overnight shift)
Add / Subtract start ± duration Wrap around 24-hour clock; report day shift
Date & time range end − start Month lengths and leap years; full calendar awareness

Converting hh:mm:ss to decimal hours

Decimal hours are useful because payroll systems multiply them by an hourly rate. The conversion is:

decimal hours = hours + (minutes ÷ 60) + (seconds ÷ 3600)

For example, 8 hours 30 minutes = 8 + (30 ÷ 60) = 8.50 decimal hours. This calculator shows decimal hours to two decimal places, which is the standard precision for most payroll and billing systems.

Converting hh:mm:ss to total minutes

Total minutes are useful for pro-rated billing, where a client is charged per minute:

total minutes = (hours × 60) + minutes + (seconds ÷ 60)

A note on daylight saving time

Duration calculations within a single time zone operate on the local time values you enter. Across a DST transition, however, one calendar day can be 23 or 25 hours long in real time — so a "24-hour" span from noon Saturday to noon Sunday might actually be 23 hours on the clock during a spring-forward transition, or 25 hours during a fall-back transition.

The Between Two Times and Add / Subtract tabs operate on clock times, so they don't know about DST. If you need DST-accurate durations, convert both timestamps to UTC first and subtract there, or use the Date & Time Range tab with full dates so that calendar-day counts stay correct.

For the vast majority of everyday use — work shifts, meeting lengths, cooking times, exercise durations — DST is irrelevant, and this calculator gives you the exact answer you need.

  • Decimal hours = 8.00

  • Result = 16:15:00, same day

Example 4: Subtracting past midnight

Start: 02:30:00 · Subtract: 4 hours

  • Result = 22:30:00, previous day
