Why Use This Word Counter?

I needed a word counter that didn't force me to sign up, didn't feel sluggish, and didn't send my text to some server in the cloud. So I built one that ticks all three boxes — and then added a few things I always wished other counters had.

Instant, live stats

No need to click a button. Every number updates as you type or paste.

Complete privacy

Your text stays on your device. I don't see it, and I don't store it.

Readability scores

Flesch Reading Ease and Flesch-Kincaid Grade Level, shown as clear numbers.

Text history

Save multiple versions locally and compare them anytime.

Works offline

Once the page loads, you can disconnect and keep working.

Multi-language support

Character counting works for any language. Word counting works for space-separated languages.

How to Use the Word Counter

  1. Paste or type your text into the box above.
  2. Watch the stats appear instantly in the sidebar.
  3. Use the toolbar inside the textarea to clear, copy, change case, or export your text.
