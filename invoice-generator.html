What does this Invoice Generator do?

This free online invoice generator lets you create professional, client-ready invoices directly in your browser — no signup, no installation and no subscription. Fill in your business details, upload your logo and signature, add line items, and the invoice preview updates live as you type. When you're happy with it, click Download PDF and the file saves straight to your device.

New here? Click Load Example and the whole form fills itself in — business details, client, four line items, tax, discount, a logo and a signature. You can see exactly what a finished invoice looks like, then edit it or clear it and start your own.

It handles everything a standard invoice needs: multiple line items, automatic quantity × rate calculation, tax percentage, discount percentage, 18+ currencies, your business branding, and a signature block. Because everything runs locally in your browser with JavaScript, your invoice data, logo and signature never leave your device.

How to create an invoice (step by step)

  1. Load an example (optional but recommended the first time): Click Load Example to see a fully filled invoice, then edit it into your own.
  2. Enter your business details: Add your business name, address, email and phone number.
  3. Upload your logo and signature (optional): Click Choose Logo and Choose Signature, or simply drag and drop an image into the upload box. The logo goes to the top-left of the invoice, above your business details; the signature goes above the signature line at the bottom.
  4. Add client information: Fill in the client's name and address in the Bill To section.
  5. Set invoice info: Enter an invoice number, invoice date and due date. Today's date is filled in automatically.
  6. Add line items: For each product or service, enter a description, quantity and rate. The amount is calculated automatically as you type.
  7. Apply tax and discount: Enter a tax percentage and/or discount percentage if applicable. The discount is applied before tax.
  8. Add notes or payment terms: Use the Notes / Terms field for bank details, UPI IDs, GSTIN / VAT numbers, late-fee terms or a simple thank-you.
  9. Download PDF: Click Download PDF and the invoice saves directly to your device. No print dialog, no extra clicks. The filename is based on your invoice number.

Invoice vs. receipt vs. quotation

These three documents look similar but do very different jobs. Using the wrong one is a common cause of payment delays.

DocumentWhen it's sentWhat it means
QuotationBefore the work starts"This is what the job will cost if you hire me." It's an offer, not a demand for payment.
InvoiceAfter the work is delivered"This is what you owe me for the work already done." It requests payment by a due date.
ReceiptAfter payment is received"I confirm I have been paid." It closes the loop and gives the client proof of payment.

In short: quote first, invoice on delivery, receipt on payment. If you send an invoice when you meant to send a quotation, clients often sit on it because they think nothing is owed yet. If you need a matching document for the earlier stages, try our Quotation Generator or Receipt Generator.

Tips to get paid faster

  • Number invoices consistently. Use a simple sequence like 2025-001, 2025-002 so nothing gets lost. (You can generate one with our Invoice Number Generator.)
  • Set a clear due date. "Due on receipt" is vague. "Due 15 March 2025" is not.
  • Shorten your payment terms. Net 30 is standard, but many freelancers use Net 7 or Net 14 and get paid noticeably sooner.
  • Put payment details on the invoice itself. Bank account, UPI ID, PayPal link — remove every excuse for delay.
  • Add late-fee terms. A line like "2% late fee applies after the due date" is often enough to keep clients on time.
  • Itemise everything. Vague invoices get queried; itemised invoices get approved.
  • Brand it. A logo and signature make the document feel official rather than a thrown-together note.
  • Send it as a PDF. PDFs look the same on every device and can't be edited by accident.
  • Follow up politely. A short reminder a day after the due date resolves a surprising number of "I forgot" situations.
