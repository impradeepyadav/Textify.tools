What does this Text Repeater do?

This free online text repeater duplicates any word, sentence, emoji, or block of text as many times as you want. Whether you need 5 copies or 100,000, you can generate them instantly without typing the same thing over and over. It is perfect for creating 1000 times copy and paste strings, emoji walls, and spam text for pranks.

Unlike basic repeaters that just paste text, this tool gives you full control over the output. You can choose the separator between copies (new line, space, comma, pipe, bullet, tab, or anything custom), add automatic numbering with the {n} placeholder, and optionally append a trailing separator for CSV-style output.

It also handles emojis perfectly. Whether you want to repeat a single emoji like 🔥 a hundred times, create a long heart string ❤️❤️❤️, or build an emoji wall for a group chat, the tool treats emojis exactly like regular text — so you can repeat them as many times as you need. Popular searches include emoji 1000 times copy and paste, 🔥 emoji 1000 times, and text art ❤️ 100000.

Everything runs locally in your browser. The text you paste never leaves your device, there's no signup, and there's no built-in limit beyond a browser safety guard that prevents runaway output from freezing your tab.

Separator guide: choosing the right one

The separator determines how each copy of your text is joined to the next. Picking the right one depends entirely on what you're going to do with the output.

SeparatorWhat it doesBest for
New lineEach copy appears on its own lineLists, test data rows, practice sheets
SpaceCopies are joined with a single spaceInline repetition, emphasis in messages
Comma + spaceCopies are separated by a comma and spaceCSV-style lists, inline data
PipeCopies are separated by a vertical barDelimited files, data exports
Bullet dotCopies are separated by a bullet characterVisual lists, social media captions
TabCopies are separated by a tab characterSpreadsheet paste, TSV data
NoneCopies are joined directly with no gapRepeating a pattern with no break (e.g., "ababab") or a solid emoji wall 🔥🔥🔥
CustomAny character or string, including \n and \tSpecial delimiters, multi-character separators

If you're building a CSV row, use "Comma + space" and enable the trailing separator. If you're generating numbered test data, use "New line" with auto-numbering enabled. For visual effects in messages, the bullet dot and space separators work best. And for a solid wall of emojis with no gaps, choose "None".

Why there's a browser safety guard

When you repeat text 100,000 times, the output can grow to tens of megabytes. That's a lot for a single text field to hold, and pushing it too far can freeze or crash your browser tab.

This tool includes a built-in safety guard that estimates the output size before generating. If the result would be unreasonably large (usually above ~5 million characters), you'll see a warning banner instead of a frozen tab. That threshold is high enough for almost any real-world use case — 100,000 repetitions of a short phrase like "Hello" fits comfortably — but low enough to catch accidental runaway inputs.
