PDF Lock

Add password protection to PDF files right in your browser. Upload your PDF, set a password, and download the encrypted version. No uploads to servers — everything happens locally on your device.

Click to upload or drag & drop a PDF file

File must be a .pdf

Why lock a PDF?

Protecting sensitive documents with a password is essential for privacy and security. This tool lets you add a password to any PDF right from your browser, without needing to install any software.

  • Privacy – prevent unauthorized access to confidential documents.
  • Security – encrypt the PDF so only those with the password can open it.
  • Compliance – meet regulatory requirements for document protection.
  • No software needed – works entirely in your browser.
  • Free and unlimited – lock as many PDFs as you need.

How to lock a PDF (step-by-step)

1. Upload PDF

Click or drag the PDF file you want to protect.

2. Enter password

Type a strong password and confirm it.

3. Click Lock

The tool will encrypt the PDF locally and remove the password.

4. Download

Save the password-protected PDF to your device.

Common use cases

  • Confidential documents – protect contracts, financial statements, and personal records.
  • Legal documents – secure sensitive legal files before sharing.
  • Business proposals – limit access to proprietary information.
  • Healthcare records – comply with privacy regulations.
  • Academic materials – protect unpublished research or exams.
