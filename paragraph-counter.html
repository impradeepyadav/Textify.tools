Why use this Paragraph Counter?

Many writing assignments, articles, and formal documents require a specific number of paragraphs. This tool not only counts paragraphs but also gives you complete text statistics at a glance. Since everything is processed locally, your content stays 100% private and secure.

  • Paragraph count – counts blocks of text separated by one or more blank lines, exactly as most word processors do.
  • Additional stats – also shows total words, sentences, and characters (with and without spaces).
  • Real‑time update – counts refresh instantly as you type or paste.
  • Privacy‑first – no data ever leaves your browser.
  • No installation – works directly in any modern browser, mobile or desktop.
  • Completely free – no hidden costs, no premium plans, no registration required.

How to count paragraphs (step‑by‑step)

1. Paste your text

Copy the text you want to analyze and drop it into the box above. You can also type directly.

2. Review the stats

The stat cards update instantly, showing paragraph count along with word, sentence, and character counts.

3. Copy if needed

Click "Copy Stats" to copy a summary of all counts to your clipboard, or "Copy Text" to copy the whole text.

4. Clear and start over

Use the Clear button to wipe the text and reset all counts to zero. Start fresh anytime.

Common use cases

Our paragraph counter is trusted by thousands of students, writers, editors, and professionals every day. Here are just a few ways it can help you:

  • Academic assignments: Check if your essay meets paragraph count guidelines before submission.
  • Content creation: Ensure blog posts or articles are broken into readable paragraphs for better engagement.
  • Editing and proofreading: Analyze structure and paragraph distribution to improve readability.
  • Legal documents: Count sections separated by line breaks in contracts or agreements.
  • Text analysis: Get a quick overview of any document's composition — useful for translators and linguists.
  • Social media posts: Check if your caption fits platform guidelines that require a certain number of paragraphs.

What makes our tool unique?

Unlike other paragraph counters that may require sign‑ups or send your data to a server, ours is 100% client‑side. That means:

  • Instant results – no page reloads, no waiting for a server response.
  • Complete privacy – your text never touches our servers.
  • Works offline – once the page is loaded, you can even disconnect the internet and keep using the tool.
  • No ads inside the tool – the text area is clean and distraction‑free.

Pro tips

  • A paragraph is defined as a block of text separated by one or more blank lines.
  • If your text uses only a single newline between paragraphs, they will be counted as a single paragraph.
  • Combine with Text Statistics for even more metrics like reading time and unique words.
  • After checking paragraph count, you might also want to use our Word Counter or Sentence Counter for focused counts.
  • Use the download feature to export your text in various formats (TXT, CSV, JSON, HTML, or PDF).
