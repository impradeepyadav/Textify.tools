Why convert PNG to JPG?

JPG is a smaller, more widely compatible format that's perfect for photos and sharing. Converting PNG to JPG can help:

  • Reduce file size – JPG compression is much more efficient than PNG for photographs.
  • Improve compatibility – JPG is supported everywhere, even on older devices.
  • Faster uploads – smaller files upload to social media or email faster.
  • Save storage space – JPG takes less room on your hard drive.
  • Prepare for web – JPG is often the preferred format for web photos.

Common use cases
