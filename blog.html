Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the questions we get most often about the blog and the tools behind it.

Are all Textify tools really free to use?
Yes. Every tool on Textify.tools is free, with no signup, no hidden limits, and no watermark. You can use them as often as you want, for personal or commercial work.
Do you store or track what I type into the tools?
No. Most tools run entirely in your browser, which means your text never leaves your device. We don't log input, and we don't sell data. The only analytics we keep are anonymous page views to see which tools are actually useful.
Can I use these tools on my phone or tablet?
Absolutely. The entire site is responsive and works on phones, tablets, laptops, and desktops. File-based tools like PDF Merge and Image Resizer also work on mobile browsers.
Which tool should I start with for cleaning messy text?
Start with the Text Cleaner for an all-in-one pass. If you know exactly what's wrong, use the specific ones: Remove Extra Spaces, Remove Empty Lines, Remove Line Breaks, or Remove Blank Characters. There's a step-by-step walkthrough in our featured guide.
Do you have an API, or do I need an account?
No account is needed for anything. There's no public API at the moment — every tool is meant to be used directly in the browser. If you need a specific integration, get in touch and we'll see what's possible.
How often do you publish new guides?
Roughly every one to two weeks. Guides are written based on what readers actually ask for, so if there's a workflow you'd like covered, feel free to send a suggestion.
