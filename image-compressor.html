The tool uses the browser's built-in Canvas API to re-encode your image. Which Format Should You Choose?

FormatCompressionBest ForTransparencyFile Size
JPEGLossy, quality slider activePhotos, complex images with gradients, web hero images, government formsNoSmall to medium
WebPLossy and lossless, best overall compressionModern websites, anywhere you can control the outputYes (lossy and lossless)Smallest
PNGLossless only, quality slider has no effectScreenshots, logos, UI mockups, images with textYesLargest

If your image has transparency and you want the smallest possible file, choose WebP. If you need broad compatibility with older software or the image is a photo without transparency, JPEG is the safest choice. Choose PNG only if you need pixel-perfect reproduction or you are compressing an already-optimized file and just want to resize it.

Image Compression Best Practices

  • Start with the highest quality original. Recompressing an already-compressed JPEG will show artifacts. Always compress from the original.
  • Pick the right format. JPEG for photos, PNG for screenshots and text, WebP when you need the best of both.
  • Resize before you compress. A 4000px-wide photo displayed at 800px wide on your site should be resized to 800px. This alone often cuts the file size by 80%.
  • Use quality 80 as a default. It is the well-known sweet spot for JPEG and WebP. Raise it for text-heavy images and lower it for small thumbnails.
  • Use the Target Size tab for strict upload limits. If a form says 50KB max, don't guess the quality — let the tool find it for you automatically.
  • Strip metadata. Camera EXIF data, GPS coordinates and thumbnails can add kilobytes to your file. Re-encoding through Canvas removes all of it.
  • Test on real devices. Image quality that looks fine on a desktop monitor may look soft on a high-DPI mobile display. Always check before shipping.
  • Consider the trade-off. Smaller file sizes mean faster pages but lower image quality. Balance these based on your audience and their connection speed.
