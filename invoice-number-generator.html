Why use this invoice number generator?

Invoice numbers are essential for organizing financial records, tracking payments, and maintaining professional communication with clients. This tool helps you create a series of invoice numbers that follow a consistent pattern, making it easier to manage your billing system.

  • Consistent formatting – ensure all invoice numbers look professional and uniform.
  • Sequential generation – avoid duplicate numbers by generating a batch in order.
  • Customizable – add prefix, separator, date, and control digit length.
  • Privacy first – all numbers are generated in your browser; nothing is uploaded.
  • Free to use – no signup, no hidden charges.

How to generate invoice numbers (step-by-step)

1. Set your preferences

Enter prefix, starting number, digits, separator, and quantity.

2. Click Generate

The tool will instantly create a list of numbers.

3. Copy them

Copy individual numbers or all at once.

4. Use in your invoices

Paste into your invoice generator or accounting software.

Common invoice number formats

  • Sequential – 0001, 0002, 0003…
  • Prefix + Sequence – INV-0001, INV-0002…
  • Date-based – 20260905-0001 (YYYYMMDD-XXXX).
  • Client code + Sequence – CLIENT-0001.
  • Year prefix – 2026-0001, 2026-0002…
