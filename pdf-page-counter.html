Why Use This PDF Page Counter?

Whether you're a student submitting an assignment, a professional preparing a report, or just curious about a large PDF, knowing the page count is a basic but essential piece of information. This tool gives you that answer in seconds without any complicated setup or privacy concerns.

How It Works

Simply drag and drop your PDF file into the box above, or click to browse your device. The tool reads the PDF structure directly in your browser using the PDF.js library. Within milliseconds, the total number of pages is displayed. No file is ever uploaded to a server – everything happens locally on your machine.

Features

  • Instant page count: Get the exact number of pages as soon as you select a file.
  • 100% private: Your PDF never leaves your device. No server-side processing.
  • No file size limit: Works with small and large PDFs alike (performance depends on your device).
  • Simple and intuitive: No confusing options – just drop, count, done.
  • Works offline: Once the page loads, you can use it without an internet connection.

Use Cases

  • Academic submissions: Ensure your PDF meets page limits for assignments or theses.
  • Professional documents: Quickly verify page counts for contracts, reports, or presentations.
  • Print preparation: Know how many pages will be printed before sending to a printer.
  • File organisation: Sort or rename PDFs based on their length.
