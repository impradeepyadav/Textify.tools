What Are Unicode Italic Fonts?

Unicode italic fonts are special character sets that use Unicode symbols to create slanted, italic, or cursive variations of regular text. Unlike standard CSS italic formatting (which only works in rich‑text environments), Unicode italic characters work everywhere – text messages, social media bios, email subject lines, and plain text documents. Each italic style comes from a different Unicode block, giving you a wide range of visual options.
