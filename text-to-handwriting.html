What does this Text to Handwriting tool do?

This free online text to handwriting converter turns any typed text into realistic handwritten pages. You get everything on one page: 11 built-in handwriting styles, custom font upload, six paper types, ink colors, alignment options, ink pressure simulation, random jitter for realism, multi-page PDF export, high-resolution PNG export, and direct printing.

There is no separate download tool, no separate font uploader, no separate print page. Everything happens right here in your browser. Type or paste your text, tune the settings, preview every page, and export or print — all without leaving the page.

Because everything runs locally with HTML5 Canvas and JavaScript, your text and any custom font you upload never leave your device. No signup, no server, no logging. Close the tab and it's gone.

How to convert text to handwriting

  1. Enter your text: Type or paste your content into the input box. Line breaks become new lines on the page.
  2. Pick a handwriting style: Choose from 11 built-in styles, or click Upload Font to use your own handwriting font (TTF/OTF/WOFF).
  3. Select paper type: Ruled, grid, plain, dotted, yellow pad, or aged paper.
  4. Choose ink color: Dark blue, black, blue, green, red, or purple.
  5. Set alignment: Left, center, right, or justified.
  6. Fine-tune spacing: Adjust font size, line height, and letter spacing.
  7. Enable realism options: Random jitter, ink pressure simulation, and baseline snap give a more natural look.
  8. Pick orientation: Portrait (default) or landscape.
  9. Click Generate Handwriting: Every page renders in the preview, with a page count.
  10. Export or print: Download PNG, download a real multi-page PDF, print directly, or open the current page in a new tab.

You can also press Ctrl + Enter (or Cmd + Enter on Mac) inside the input box to generate instantly.

Tips for the most realistic handwriting

  • Upload your own handwriting font for the most personal result. Scan or find a TTF/OTF of your own handwriting and upload it here.
  • Use a casual or cursive style — "Neat Cursive" and "Casual Print" tend to look the most natural.
  • Stick to blue or black ink for assignments. Green, red, and purple are better for creative work.
  • Keep font size moderate — 22–26px works best for most handwriting.
  • Use line height around 1.4–1.8 for realistic spacing.
  • Enable jitter and ink pressure for a more natural look — real handwriting is never perfectly uniform.
  • Break text into short paragraphs. Long walls of text look less natural than broken-up paragraphs.
  • Use ruled paper for assignments and plain or grid paper for creative projects.

How the rendering works

This tool uses HTML5 Canvas and web fonts to render your text. Here's what happens when you click Generate:

  • A canvas is created at 2× resolution for crisp output
  • The selected paper background is drawn (ruled lines, grid, dots, yellow pad, or aged texture)
  • Your text is word-wrapped to fit within page margins
  • Each line is drawn with the chosen font, ink color, alignment, size, and spacing
  • If jitter is enabled, each line gets a small random rotation and offset
  • If ink pressure is enabled, opacity is varied slightly per character for realism
  • If baseline snap is enabled, each line is nudged onto the ruled paper baseline
  • Long text is automatically split into multiple A4 pages, each rendered the same way

PNG export captures the current page at full resolution. PDF export uses jsPDF to embed every rendered page into a real multi-page A4 PDF. Print opens your browser's print dialog with a print-ready version of all pages.
