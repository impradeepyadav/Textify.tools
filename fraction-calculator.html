What does this fraction calculator do?

This free online fraction calculator performs all four basic arithmetic operations on fractions and mixed numbers: addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division. It returns the result in three forms at once — simplified fraction, mixed number, and decimal — along with a step-by-step breakdown that shows exactly how the answer was derived.

Fractions show up everywhere: recipes, measurements, financial ratios, probability, and of course math homework. But doing fraction arithmetic by hand is error-prone. Getting a common denominator, remembering to flip the second fraction when dividing, simplifying at the end — one small mistake and the whole answer is wrong. This calculator handles all of that automatically, so you can focus on the problem rather than the mechanics.

The tool supports proper fractions (like 3/4), improper fractions (like 7/3), mixed numbers (like 2 1/3), and whole numbers (like 5, which is just 5/1). Negative values are also supported. Everything runs locally in your browser — no data is sent to a server.

How to use the fraction calculator

  1. Enter the first fraction: Type the numerator and denominator. If it's a mixed number, also enter the whole number part (e.g. for 2 1/3, enter 2 in "Whole", 1 in "Numerator", and 3 in "Denominator").
  2. Choose the operation: Select add (+), subtract (−), multiply (×), or divide (÷).
  3. Enter the second fraction: Same as the first — numerator, denominator, and an optional whole number.
  4. Click Calculate: The result appears instantly in three forms, along with a step-by-step breakdown.
  5. Switch to Simplify mode: To simplify a single fraction or convert between fractions and decimals, use the second tab.

Fraction formulas explained

Here are the standard formulas for the four basic operations on fractions. These are the formulas this calculator uses under the hood.

Addition (with different denominators)
a/b + c/d = (a × d + c × b) / (b × d)

After adding, simplify by dividing the numerator and denominator by their GCD.
Subtraction (with different denominators)
a/b − c/d = (a × d − c × b) / (b × d)

The same denominator rule applies: find a common denominator, subtract the numerators, then simplify.
Multiplication
a/b × c/d = (a × c) / (b × d)

Multiply the numerators together and the denominators together, then simplify.
Division
a/b ÷ c/d = a/b × d/c = (a × d) / (b × c)

Multiply the first fraction by the reciprocal of the second (flip the second fraction), then simplify.
Simplifying a fraction
Find the greatest common divisor (GCD) of the numerator and denominator, then divide both by it.

Example: 12/18 → GCD(12, 18) = 6 → 12÷6 / 18÷6 = 2/3

Common fraction mistakes to avoid

  • Adding denominators directly. When adding 1/2 + 1/3, you do not add the denominators (2 + 3 = 5). You find a common denominator first: 3/6 + 2/6 = 5/6.
  • Forgetting to flip when dividing. Dividing by a fraction is the same as multiplying by its reciprocal. So 3/5 ÷ 1/2 = 3/5 × 2/1 = 6/5.
  • Not simplifying the final answer. A result of 6/15 should be simplified to 2/5 by dividing both parts by their GCD (3).
  • Mixing up the numerator and denominator. The numerator is the top number (how many parts you have), and the denominator is the bottom number (how many parts the whole is divided into).
  • Ignoring signs. A negative fraction can have the minus sign on the numerator, the denominator, or in front of the whole fraction — they all mean the same thing. The calculator handles signs correctly.
