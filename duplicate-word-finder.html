Why use this Duplicate Word Finder?

Repetitive words can weaken your writing, bore readers, or indicate areas that need revision. This tool instantly shows you which words you're overusing, along with exact counts and percentages. With visual bars you can compare frequencies at a glance, and the stop-word filter helps you focus on meaningful content words rather than common function words.

Visual bars

Quickly identify the most overused words by the length of the bar.

Stop-word exclusion

Filter out words like "the", "a", "and" to see real duplicates.

Minimum count filter

Show only words that appear at least N times.

Percentage column

See each duplicate's share of total words.

Sortable columns

How to find duplicate words (step-by-step)

  1. Paste your text — Drop any block of text — an article, blog post, or essay — into the input box.
  2. Adjust settings — Toggle case sensitivity, exclude stop words, and set a minimum occurrence count.
  3. Review the table — Each duplicate word shows its count, percentage of total words, and a visual bar.
  4. Highlight or export — Toggle highlighted text to see duplicates in context, or download a CSV.

Common use cases

  • Writing improvement: Spot overused filler words like "very", "really", or "that".
  • SEO optimization: Ensure target keywords appear enough times without crossing into keyword stuffing.
  • Academic editing: Check for unintended repetition in research papers or theses.
  • Code comment cleanup: Identify redundant terms in documentation or inline comments.
  • Text analysis: Get a quick frequency overview for any corpus of text.

Pro tips for better duplicate word analysis

  • Always enable the stop-word filter to get rid of "the", "a", "and" — they will otherwise dominate the list and hide meaningful words.
  • Adjust the minimum count to find words repeated at least 3, 4, or 5 times for a more focused view.
  • Use the percentage column to compare relative weights; a word with 5% density might be overused.
  • Combine with our Keyword Counter for a full frequency breakdown of every word.
  • After identifying duplicates you want to remove, use the Text Finder & Replacer to batch‑replace them.
