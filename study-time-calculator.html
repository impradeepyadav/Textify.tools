Why use this study time calculator?

Effective studying isn't about spending more hours — it's about allocating time wisely. This tool helps you see exactly how your study time is distributed, so you can make informed adjustments.

  • Subject-wise allocation – assign different hours to each subject.
  • Total time tracking – see your total planned study hours at a glance.
  • Per-day recommendation – know how much to study each day to finish your plan.
  • Visual breakdown – percentage bars show how time is split.
  • 100% private – everything stays in your browser.

How to plan your study schedule

1. Add subjects

Click "Add Subject" and enter the name and hours you want to study for each.

2. Set your constraints

Enter how many days you have and how many hours per day you can study.

3. Review and adjust

See the breakdown and adjust subject hours until your plan is realistic.
